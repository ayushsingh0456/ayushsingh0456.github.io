01 About

I sit between clients, product and engineering, and make sure everyone is building the same thing.

I've worked on 15+ web and mobile products across healthcare, fintech, fitness, hospitality and marketplaces, taking them from the first discovery call to live deployment. My strongest work is in regulated, operations-heavy SaaS, where a vague rule turns into a payroll error or a compliance gap.

Day to day I lead discovery, research compliance early, write BRDs and SRS documents, break scope into user stories with testable acceptance criteria, draw the system and process diagrams, and run sprints and UAT through to sign-off. When something breaks in production, I document the incident and turn what we learned into requirement changes.

I also work fluently with AI. I write specifications for AI coding agents, prototype interfaces with Claude and Figma, and use spec-driven development (GitHub Spec Kit, Amazon Kiro) to keep generated work traceable to requirements.
