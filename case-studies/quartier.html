Quartier: one app for the cafés, salons and spas on your street

A German-first platform where Vienna's independent cafés, bakeries, salons and spas take pickup orders and appointments through one customer app, one business portal and one backend. Analyzed end to end, from discovery interviews to post-incident reviews.

The hard parts are where money, capacity and safety meet: a basket mixing 10% and 20% VAT with a voucher, a pickup slot that fills while a bank challenge is still running, and an allergen declaration that must never be stale when someone orders.

02My role and approach

I ran the full lifecycle as the sole BA and wrote every artifact to the standard a delivery team would review it against. Each phase lists the documents it produced; click one to open it.

01 · DISCOVER

Discover

Interviews with 14 businesses and 22 customers, a survey of 410 residents, measured baselines, personas, AS-IS and TO-BE flows and a market scan.

02 · DEFINE

Define

A BRD with six objectives and an explicit out-of-scope list, a 29148 SRS with 76 FRs, 45 rules in decision tables, 27 NFRs and a compliance mapping.

03 · DESIGN

Design

C4 architecture, three ADRs, ERD and data dictionary, sequence diagrams, state machines, five wireframes and a 42-operation OpenAPI contract.

04 · BUILD

Build

Seven two-week sprints for R1: 38 stories in 6 epics with 166 Gherkin criteria, change control and a weekly rule clinic.

05 · TEST AND UAT

Test and UAT

A risk-based test strategy with 98 test cases, the worked examples as boundary tests, and 10 UAT scripts signed off with pilot businesses.

06 · LAUNCH AND OPERATE

Launch and operate

A pilot with 38 businesses, then the city launch; two blameless reviews produced CR-005 and CR-006 for release 1.1.

05Key decisions

These are the calls I would expect to defend in a design review.

Allergens are a publishing gate, not a disclaimer

No food item can be ordered without a complete, confirmed declaration of the 14 EU allergens, and every change creates a new version (BR-011, BR-013). Discovery had suggested showing "information not available, ask the business" instead.

The POS view is an order board, not a cash register

Taking counter payments would have brought Quartier under Austrian cash register rules while every pilot business already had a certified register, so CR-003 was rejected and staff only confirm payment at the counter.

Authorize at placement, capture at acceptance

The card is authorized when the customer orders and captured only when the business accepts, so a rejected or timed-out order costs nobody anything (ADR-003).

A hold is a courtesy; the database decides

Slot holds live in Redis with a time-to-live, but capacity is enforced by the database and re-checked when payment is authorized, with alternative slots offered if the slot filled up (ADR-002, CR-006).

07Worked example

One calculation runs through the whole document set with identical numbers: the SRS, the business rules, a story's acceptance criteria, the test cases and the test data.

A mixed-VAT basket with a voucher, and a salon start-time calculationSame numbers in every artifact
Two Topfengolatsche at EUR 3.40 and a sandwich at EUR 6.90 (10% VAT) plus a lemonade at EUR 5.00 (20% VAT) come to EUR 18.70. The EUR 3.00 voucher (minimum EUR 15.00, so EUR 14.99 is rejected) is allocated pro rata as EUR 2.20 and EUR 0.80, giving VAT of EUR 1.05 and EUR 0.70 and a total of EUR 15.70. The 8% commission is EUR 1.26 plus EUR 0.25 VAT, so the business is paid EUR 14.19.

For salons: with a 09:00 to 13:00 shift, an existing 10:30 to 11:15 appointment and a 45-minute cut plus a 15-minute buffer, the app offers 7 start times and excludes 12:15 because of the buffer.

10Reflection

59files, with 518 internal links and 36 diagrams validated
0requirements without a test case
2worked examples with identical numbers in every artifact

What worked. Writing the money and capacity rules as decision tables before any screen was drawn. The two worked examples became boundary tests, and the incident reviews could point at the exact rule that needed to change.

What I'd do next. Validate the salon flow with more stylists before Release 2, measure the onboarding target with a larger cohort, and add classes and events for spas, which were deliberately moved out of Release 1.
