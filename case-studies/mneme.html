Mneme: one searchable memory for project delivery

On services projects, context leaks into meeting recordings, chat threads, emails and people's heads. Mneme is my concept for an AI operational workspace that captures meetings, daily logs, decisions and client threads, links them to projects, and answers "what happened and why?" with sources.

01The problem

As a BA, I own requirements, decisions and change control, but the evidence behind them is scattered. A decision is made in a call, confirmed in a WhatsApp thread, and half-remembered in a spreadsheet. When a client asks why something was built a certain way, or a new team member joins mid-sprint, someone has to rebuild the story by hand.

Problem statement

Delivery teams lose time and trust because project context is fragmented across tools, and nothing connects a decision to the conversation, meeting or log that produced it.

02Users and jobs to be done

UserJob to be done
Business Analyst or Project ManagerTrack decisions, blockers and change requests across projects, and answer "what happened and why" in minutes.
Delivery leadSee project health and what needs attention today without chasing updates.
Team memberLog progress, blockers and tomorrow's plan in under 30 seconds.

03The product

I prototyped 12 connected screens around a single project record.

TODAY

Needs attention

Live meetings, pending logs, tasks, AI insights and the latest signals.

PROJECTS

Project health

Team, blockers, pending logs, and tabs for meetings, logs, communications, files and timeline.

MEETINGS

From call to record

Transcript upload, minutes, decisions, action items and an AI summary.

DAILY LOGS

30-second capture

Done, blockers and tomorrow, with auto-saved drafts.

COMMUNICATIONS

Threads that matter

Slack, Teams, WhatsApp and email threads, linked to a project.

ASSISTANT

Answers with sources

Questions answered from meetings, logs and threads, with citations.

A timeline puts every meeting, log, decision, blocker and upload for a project into one searchable history. Analytics show activity and project health over time.

04Design principles

Capture must be cheap

If a daily log takes more than 30 seconds, people stop writing them. The form has three short fields and keyboard shortcuts.

Decisions are first-class records

They are not lines buried in meeting notes. Each one is linked to its source, so it can be searched and traced later.

Every answer cites its sources

The assistant shows which meetings, logs and threads it used, so users verify rather than trust blindly.

05How I built it

I wrote the concept, user jobs and screen requirements first, then used AI-assisted prototyping to turn them into a working React interface. One consistent mock dataset feeds every screen, so links between projects, meetings and logs behave like the real product would. The result is something stakeholders can click through, which is a far better conversation starter than a slide.

06What's next

  • Validate the problem with interviews with BAs, PMs and delivery leads before building a backend.
  • Add authentication, workspaces and a data model for projects, meetings, logs, decisions and messages.
  • Add Slack, Teams and email integrations, and transcript ingestion with retrieval-augmented search for the assistant.
  • Add role-based access and audit logging, because project memory often holds client-confidential information.
